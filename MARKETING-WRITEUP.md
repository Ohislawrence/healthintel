## 1. Elevator Pitch

**HealthIntel turns confusing medical lab reports into clear, plain-language explanations in seconds — and gives people a full set of everyday tools to track and understand their health.**

Millions of people receive lab results, CT scans, ultrasound reports, and ECG printouts every day and have **no idea what they mean**. HealthIntel uses AI to read those reports — whether they're numeric lab tables or narrative imaging/scanner reports — and explains them like a trusted friend would, in the language the user understands best.

---

## 2. What The App Does (In Plain Terms)

HealthIntel is a personal health companion that helps people:

- **Upload, photograph, or manually enter** their lab results.
- **Get an instant, easy-to-read AI explanation** of what those results mean.
- **See their numbers visually** with charts and color-coded gauges (normal, borderline, critical).
- **Read results in their own language** — English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, or Nigerian Pidgin.
- **Ask follow-up questions** about their results in a conversational chat.
- **Download a clean report card** to share with their doctor or keep for their records.
- **Track everyday health** with calculators, logs, and reminder tools.
- **Find nearby providers** (labs, hospitals, specialists, insurance) when they need care.

The goal is simple: **turn medical jargon into understanding, and anxiety into action.**

## 4. Core Feature: AI Lab Interpretation

This is the app's flagship capability. It is designed to accept **any** type of result a person might bring home.

### 4.1 Three Easy Ways To Submit Results

| Input method | How it works |
|---|---|
| 📄 **Upload a PDF** | Drag-and-drop or browse for a lab report PDF. The app reads the text automatically. |
| 📸 **Take a photo** | Snap a picture of a paper lab report (or upload an image from the gallery). AI "vision" extracts the values. |
| ✍️ **Enter values manually** | Type values into structured test panels if no document is available. |

A guided **two-step flow** (Upload → Review) means users always confirm what was found before anything is interpreted.

### 4.2 What It Can Read

HealthIntel handles **both** kinds of medical reports:

- **Numeric lab tables** — Haemoglobin, glucose, cholesterol, liver/kidney panels, and more. Values are matched against reference ranges.
- **Narrative / imaging reports** — CT scans, MRI (e.g., lumbar spine), ultrasound scans, ECG/echocardiogram, Doppler studies, X-rays, and obstetric reports. The AI reads the prose findings and explains them in simple language.

This is a key differentiator: most tools only handle tidy numeric tables. HealthIntel explains **full scanner and imaging reports too**.

### 4.3 Plain-Language Explanations

Every interpretation is structured into scannable sections:

- **🩺 What This Report Is** — identifies the type of scan/report and body area.
- **⚠️ Key Findings** — abnormal or notable items first, each explained in one sentence.
- **✅ Normal Findings** — reassurance about what's fine.
- **💡 What This Means** — 2–4 simple sentences connecting the dots.
- **📋 Next Steps** — practical, non-prescriptive suggestions ("discuss with your doctor", "repeat in a few months").
- **ℹ️ Disclaimer** — always included for safety.

Explanations are written in **very simple, grade 5–6 English**, with every medical term explained in parentheses (e.g., "stenosis (narrowing)").

> **Safety-first:** HealthIntel never claims to diagnose, never prescribes medication, and always encourages users to consult a licensed healthcare professional. It flags potentially urgent findings to encourage prompt medical attention.

### 4.4 Multi-Language Support

Users can translate their interpretation into:

- 🇬🇧 **English**
- 🇳🇬 **Nigerian Pidgin (PCM)**
- 🇳🇬 **Yoruba**
- 🇳🇬 **Hausa**
- 🇳🇬 **Igbo**

A clean language selector at the top of the results page switches the explanation instantly, making the product genuinely inclusive for Nigerian audiences.

### 4.5 Visual Charts — "Results At A Glance"

For numeric results, HealthIntel renders:

- **Radial gauges** — show each value on a color-coded scale.
- **Range bars** — visualize where the value falls between low, normal, and high.
- **Distribution donut** — a summary view showing the proportion of normal vs. abnormal results.

Color coding (green = normal, amber = borderline, red = critical) gives users an instant visual answer before they even read the text.

### 4.6 Ask Questions About Your Results

A built-in **"Ask about results" chat** lets users have a conversation with an AI assistant about their specific results:

- "What does this high number mean?"
- "Should I be worried about this value?"
- "What should I ask my doctor?"

The assistant answers in plain language and always ends with a medical disclaimer.

### 4.7 Download Report Card

Users can **download a shareable PDF report card** of their interpretation — perfect for:

- Sharing with a doctor or specialist.
- Keeping for personal medical records.
- Sending to a family member or caregiver.

## 5. Health Tools

Beyond lab interpretation, HealthIntel includes a full suite of everyday health tools. These feed into the user's health profile, which makes interpretations and recommendations **more personalized**.

### Calculators
- **BMI Calculator** — body mass index.
- **BMR & TDEE Calculator** — basal metabolic rate & daily energy needs.
- **Due Date Calculator** — estimate pregnancy due date and track weeks.
- **Waist-to-Hip Ratio** — assess body fat distribution and health risk.
- **Body Fat Calculator** — estimate body fat percentage.

### Trackers & Logs
- **Blood Pressure Log** — log systolic/diastolic readings and see trends.
- **Water Intake Tracker** — set daily goals and track progress.
- **Food & Symptom Diary** — spot patterns between what you eat and how you feel.
- **Period & Cycle Tracker** — log periods, track cycles, predict ovulation & fertile windows.
- **Sleep Tracker** — monitor sleep over time.
- **Immunization Tracker** — track vaccines for both children and adults.
- **Appointment Tracker** — book, track, and get reminded about medical appointments.
- **Medication Reminder** — get reminders to take medication at the right time.

These tools position HealthIntel not just as a "one-time report reader", but as a **daily health companion** users return to.
